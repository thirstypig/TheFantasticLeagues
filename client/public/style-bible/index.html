Chosen: Sofia Sans. Regular Sofia Sans for text, Sofia Sans Condensed for headings, team names on tight rows, and big numbers like the clock. The other five stay here for comparison.

Text sizes

Four, not five.

Three is too few for a stats app: you need a size for reading, a smaller one for table cells and second lines, and a caption size for labels and chips, plus one big size for the page title. Section headings use the body size in bold, so they don't need their own size. Big apps use more (iPhone's own system has 11), but they have far more kinds of screens than we do.

Colors

Nine colors. Use the Theme buttons at the top to see both sets. Rule: no box ever has the same color as what it sits on. Cards sit on the page; search boxes and pickers use their own Field color (white in light, darker than the page in dark) with a stronger border.

What changed from the first draft

Text and Muted looked the same, so Muted is now clearly lighter. Warning and Bad looked the same, so they're one color now, Alert. Cards are much lighter than the page, so boxes stand out.

Boxes and room

Don't

Fixed-width boxes with small text that wraps.

🔍 Search NL free agents…Choose a player ›+ Another moveNo moves

Do

Boxes grow to fit the words. One line, full size.

🔍 Search NL free agentsChoose a player to drop ›
  • Anything you tap is at least 44px tall. Main buttons are 48px.
  • List rows are at least 56px tall.
  • Buttons and chips never wrap. If the words don't fit, the layout stacks, not the text.
  • No fixed pixel widths on boxes. A desktop column holding a form is at least 340px wide.
  • No box has the same background as what's behind it. Cards on the page, Field color for anything you type in or pick from.
  • Gaps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24px. Corners: 12px. Chips are fully round.
  • No page repeats the league name, season or phase banner. The header shows it once.
  • Desktop pages fit on one screen where they can, by using two columns instead of one long one.

Auction

What the auction has today
  • It works: live bidding over a connection that updates instantly, a 15-second bid clock that resets on every bid, max bids that bid for you, a nomination queue, pause and resume, and a results page with grades.
  • No phone layout. On a phone it squeezes the computer page into the top half of the screen, so the bid buttons can scroll out of view.
  • You can't see other teams' money while bidding. Budgets and max bids live on a separate Teams tab, so you leave the player list to check them.
  • Tiny text: 8 to 11px on the bid clock, the tabs and the player table. Five tabs at 11px don't fit a phone.
  • Bid buttons are only +$1 and +$5, with no way to type an amount.
  • Commissioner gaps: you can't pause while someone is choosing a player, and some controls may need the site admin, not the commissioner.
  • Dead weight: about 2,900 lines of old copies nothing uses, and a "classic" page that is the same as the new one.

Plan: keep the engine, rebuild the screens. The screens below cover the whole auction.
